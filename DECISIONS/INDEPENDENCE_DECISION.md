## 1. Decision

**AetherHuman is classified as (E) Undetermined (interim).**

The evidence is insufficient to accept AetherHuman as an independent architectural dimension, and insufficient to reject it. A test is needed to decide.

---

## 2. Why Insufficient to Accept

| # | Reason |
|:-:|---|
| 1 | H-F4 is untested: no benefit has been observed. |
| 2 | H-F2 remainder may be small. |
| 3 | H-F3 has residuals (veto, mandatory intervention). |
| 4 | P1 is near-trivial; P2 cuts against independence. |
| 5 | AetherGovern and AetherIdentity are themselves unresolved. |

---

## 3. Why Insufficient to Reject

| # | Reason |
|:-:|---|
| 1 | No failure condition has been falsified. |
| 2 | The comparison found a remainder after composition with the neighbours (H-F2). |
| 3 | The decentralization argument did not reach a requirement for a central decision point (H-F3). |
| 4 | The rejection would rest on the same author's reading that is here judged insufficient to accept. |

---

## 4. What "Undetermined" Means

"Undetermined" is not a failure. It states that the evidence is insufficient to decide, and names what is missing.

The Phase 4 decision is **interim** because Phase 2 (Independent Review) has not been completed.

---

## 5. What Would Change the Decision

To move from (E) interim to (A) or (R), one of the following is needed:

1. **Phase 2 completion** — an independent review of the hypothesis, the boundaries, and the plan.
2. **A stronger argument** addressing H-F2 (the most fragile condition).
3. **An experiment** (requires Phase 5 and 6, which are not permitted).

Without one of these, the project remains at (E) interim.

---

## 6. The Four Other Possible Outcomes

| Outcome | Meaning | Consequence |
|:-:|---|---|
| (A) Accept | Survives all four failure conditions. | Phase 5 permitted. |
| (R) Reject | At least one condition falsified. | Candidate closed. |
| (F) Fold | All functions fold into existing dimensions. | Candidate absorbed. |
| (D) Defer | Review cannot be completed for an external reason. | Remains at Phase 4. |
| (E) Undetermined | Cannot falsify, cannot measure benefit. | Remains at Phase 4. |

The current outcome is **(E)**.

---

## 7. What This Decision Does Not Claim

- That AetherHuman is independent.
- That the five functions are complete.
- That the boundaries are sufficient.
- That the gap is genuine.
- That implementation should begin.
