───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  CENTRAL QUESTION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  How can human participation — as principal, overseer, or mandatory
  intervention point — be represented in a decentralized autonomous agent
  ecosystem, without reducing it to delegated authority or breaking the
  autonomy assumptions of the existing dimensions?

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FIVE FUNCTIONS (INITIAL SCOPE)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Fn1 — Human Principal          How is a human represented as an
       Representation            architectural principal (not merely an
                                 agent of a special type)?
  Fn2 — Authority Relation       What authority can a human exercise over
                                 autonomous agents — delegated, oversight,
                                 veto, or independent?
  Fn3 — Intervention Semantics   How does human intervention affect
                                 execution — optional, conditional, or
                                 mandatory non-bypassable?
  Fn4 — Accountability Chain     How is human responsibility attributed and
                                 traced across agent delegations?
  Fn5 — Human-Agent Boundary     How is the boundary between human decision
                                 and agent autonomy maintained without
                                 collapsing one into the other?

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  ARCHITECTURAL HYPOTHESIS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  A potentially independent architectural gap may exist between governance
  authority, identity, and runtime monitoring: the operational representation
  of human participation — its principal status, authority relations,
  intervention semantics, accountability, and boundary with agent autonomy
  — across decentralized autonomous agents.

  This hypothesis is falsifiable.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FALSIFICATION CONDITIONS (H-F1 TO H-F4)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  H-F1  Non-redundancy            Not falsified (with reservation)
  H-F2  Distinction from          Not falsified (with reservation) -- MOST FRAGILE
        Govern + Identity + Runtime
  H-F3  Decentralizability        Not falsified (with reservation)
  H-F4  Observable benefit        Untested

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  INDEPENDENCE DECISION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Classification : (E) undetermined

  The argument is insufficient to accept AetherHuman as an independent
  architectural dimension, and insufficient to reject it. A test is needed.

  The project is paused at Phase 4. Phase 5 (Specification) is not started.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  WHAT THIS PROJECT DOES NOT CLAIM
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  - That AetherHuman is an independent architectural dimension.
  - That the five functions are complete.
  - That the boundaries with AetherGovern, AetherIdentity, AetherRuntime,
    and the other dimensions are sufficient.
  - That the gap is genuine.
  - That implementation should begin.
