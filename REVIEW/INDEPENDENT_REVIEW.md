## 2. Five Questions

### Q1 — Is the central question clear and well-scoped?

**Reviewer's answer:** Yes. The central question successfully limits its scope to decentralized multi-agent ecosystems. It clearly distinguishes the human role (principal, overseer, mandatory intervention) from existing dimensions like governance or identity. The four clauses tying to the failure conditions provide a rigorous boundary for the investigation.

### Q2 — Are the five functions complete, independent, and necessary?

**Reviewer's answer:** The functions (Fn1–Fn5) capture the essential surface of human participation. However, Fn4 (Accountability Chain) appears to have a high degree of overlap with AetherAudit. It may not be structurally independent, as the paper's boundary analysis honestly admits. Fn1 (Human Principal) and Fn3 (Intervention Semantics) remain the strongest candidates for architectural independence, provided their state transitions remain strictly decoupled from automated governance overrides.

### Q3 — Are the four failure conditions (H-F1 to H-F4) testable?

**Reviewer's answer:** H-F1, H-F2, and H-F3 are testable by logical argument and structural mapping. H-F4 (Observable benefit) requires the proposed evaluation design (S1–S6) to be executed. Without executed experiments, H-F4 remains strictly untested. This is acceptable for Phase 4 but correctly blocks progression to Phase 5.

### Q4 — Is the evaluation design (S1–S6, M1–M6) sufficient to test the conditions?

**Reviewer's answer:** The design is logically sound. The mapping of six scenarios (S1–S6) to specific metrics (M1–M6) covers the failure conditions well. However, S5 (Adversarial human) will need more detailed threat modeling—specifically addressing Sybil delegation and latency-induced race conditions—before it can be executed effectively.

### Q5 — Is the Phase 4 decision (E) Undetermined (interim) justified?

**Reviewer's answer:** Yes. The paper correctly resists the temptation to claim independence without empirical evidence (H-F4). Keeping the status at (E) Undetermined (interim) aligns perfectly with the falsification-first methodology.
